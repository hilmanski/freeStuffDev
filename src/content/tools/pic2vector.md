Pic2Vector converts PNG, JPG, and WebP images into scalable SVG vector graphics entirely in your browser. The tracer is based on open-source vtracer compiled to WebAssembly and runs on your device. Choose full color, black and white, or posterize modes, tune with live sliders, and export a watermark-free SVG. Free for the core converter with no sign-up.
