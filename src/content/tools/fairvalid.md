300 free credits every month, shared between email verification and FairMatch work-email discovery.
Check one email address or a CSV list, preserving the original rows and columns on export.
Valid or invalid verification results cost 1 credit; unknown and catch-all results have zero net credit cost. Each newly delivered verified FairMatch work email costs 4 credits.
Server API and hosted MCP access are available. Test keys support reserved example addresses without using credits.
Optional paid credit packs are available; the monthly free allowance is not a time-limited trial.
