AI Metadata Cleaner is a free, 100% client-side privacy and metadata hygiene utility that inspects and removes embedded C2PA Content Credentials (JUMBF manifests), IPTC DigitalSourceType declarations, XMP history, EXIF camera tags, and generator parameters from Midjourney, DALL·E / GPT-4o, Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, Adobe Firefly, Sora, Runway, and Veo files. It also provides a dedicated [free photo and video GPS coordinates remover](https://aimetadatacleaner.pro/remove-gps) and batch processing up to 50 files at once without ever uploading your files to a server.
