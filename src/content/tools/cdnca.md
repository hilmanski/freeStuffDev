AGPL-3.0 Community Edition with a currently free control plane and no activation requirement.

Manage your own edge nodes, websites and origins, cache policies and refresh/preheat tasks, DNS and certificates, WAF/CC policies, traffic statistics and logs.

Operators supply their own servers, IP addresses and bandwidth. Optional paid plugins and CDNCA Plus are separate offerings.
