Free forever. No limits on the free tier.

- A hosted page at kulhad.co/yourname with your UPI QR code
- Unlimited supporters
- Thank-you wall, with manual approval before anything publishes
- Printable QR code
- Export your supporter list as CSV
- 7 languages (English, Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam)
- No KYC or business account needed

0% commission. Payments go bank to bank over UPI, so the money
never passes through the platform and there is nothing to withdraw.

India only, UPI needs an Indian bank account on both ends.

Paid tier (₹99/mo) adds custom domain, themes, multiple UPI IDs,
voice notes and page analytics.
