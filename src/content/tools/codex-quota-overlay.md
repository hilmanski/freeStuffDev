Free and open-source utility for Windows 10/11 x64 Codex Desktop users.
Displays quota remaining, reset countdown, and reset-credit details beside the conversation title, with optional recent burn-rate context.
Includes a local Quota Center with pace, forecast, history, and activity views.
This is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.
